title: Quilt
---

Ear held to Sylvia's ribcage
<br>I hear her heart beat, lungs breathe.
<br>Like murmuring river, red water,
<br>like whispering spring, warm breeze.
<br>
<br>The persimmons will fall from their branches;
<br>the autumn leaves go last. 
<br>The blood will wash out of the mattress.
<br>The tiger's year will pass.
<br>
<br>Our hair will turn gray with the seasons
<br>Creases will fold by our eyes.
<br>We'll set our alarms every evening
<br>Send stickers before our goodbyes.
<br>
<br>I've woven a quilt with our memories
<br>and bundled us in it tonight.
<br>I'll listen to Sylvia's heartbeat
<br>And sleep until morning's first light.
