You tell me. If I use my inside voice<br>
this time around, will I stop wanting<br>
to crawl out of my skin? <br>
<br>
If I chew before I swallow, swallow before<br>
I speak, will I feel satisfied with<br>
protein bars and Cup Ramen?<br>
<br>
If I make sure to shut the window<br>
when it snows, will it keep me from<br>
drowning beneath forty-second street again?<br>
<br>
They say "mother knows best" but you haven't seen<br>
what's happened since you let me go. I reach<br>
for the handlebars but find burnt-out cigarette,<br>
calloused hands, half-dead vape. I taste burnt<br>
metal. When I get started I can pick my scabs <br>
forever. Two more missed calls. <br>
<br>
I'm tearing a hole<br>
in the fabric between my-<br>
self and my mother, I'm<br>
starving for summer fruit<br>
in the dead of winter. <br>
<br>
I yell relentless <br>
beneath flickering streetlamp, waiting, waiting,<br>
waiting, for the one February snowstorm<br>
that will swallow me whole.
