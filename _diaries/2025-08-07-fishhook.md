i don't care anymore. the fish squirms on its hook. barbed wire through eye. in boy scouts i was once taught how to remove the fishhook from a finger. "it's barbed -- it'll drive through your skin if you pull back," says the scoutmaster surrounded by boys. "best to push it all the way through before you cut it out."
